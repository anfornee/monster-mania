# Core Rules v1.2

## Objective

Players compete to defeat Monsters and earn points.

When all regular Monsters have been defeated, each player totals the point values of the Monsters they collected.

The player with the most points wins.

If two or more players are tied for the highest score, they enter Sudden Death against the designated Sudden Death Monster.

## Standard Game Components

A standard game uses:

* 18 Weapon cards
* 5 Action cards
* 1 Ultimate Weapon card
* 17 regular Monster cards
* 1 dedicated Sudden Death Monster

The 18 Weapon cards, 5 Action cards, and 1 Ultimate Weapon are shuffled together to form a **24-card shared Draw deck**.

Expansions may introduce new cards that can be mixed into the game, but a standard game still uses the same category totals unless an expansion specifically states otherwise.

The exact identities of the cards may change. The 17 regular Monsters also do not need to use the same distribution of point values in every deck. A deck may contain more 2-point Monsters, fewer 1-point Monsters, or another supported distribution while still containing exactly 17 regular Monsters.

The core game uses:

* Draw 1 and Draw 2 as its Action cards
* Black Hole as its Ultimate Weapon
* The Infinity Beast as its Sudden Death Monster

## Shared Draw Deck

All players draw from one shared Draw deck.

At the beginning of the game, each player draws 3 cards.

Players may normally have no more than 5 cards in their hand.

Used cards are placed into a shared discard pile unless a card specifically states otherwise.

## Monster Board

Two regular Monster cards are normally face-up and available to defeat.

Each Monster shows the exact combination of Weapon cards required to defeat it.

In the core game, Monster difficulty follows:

* 1-point Monster requires 2 Weapon cards
* 2-point Monster requires 3 Weapon cards
* 3-point Monster requires 4 Weapon cards

The number of Monsters at each point value may vary between compatible decks or expansions. Only the total of 17 regular Monsters is fixed by the standard game format.

When a Monster is defeated:

1. The player collects that Monster.
2. Cards used to defeat it are placed in the shared discard pile unless a card says otherwise.
3. A new Monster is drawn to replace it when normal replacement rules allow.

A player may defeat only one Monster per turn.

## Final Three Monsters

When only 3 undefeated regular Monsters remain, all 3 are placed face-up on the board.

From this point onward, defeated Monsters are not replaced.

The board shrinks from 3 Monsters to 2, then 1, then 0 as the remaining Monsters are defeated.

## Player Turn

A normal turn has two stages.

### 1. Action Phase

The player may play any number of Action cards from their hand.

Action cards resolve one at a time.

After an Action card resolves, the player may play another Action card, including an Action card they just received from a previous Action.

There is no normal limit to the number of Action cards that may be played during the Action Phase.

Once the player begins playing Weapon or Ultimate Weapon cards to defeat a Monster, their Action Phase is over for that turn.

### 2. Resolve the Turn

After finishing their Action Phase, the player must either:

* Defeat one Monster, or
* Skip their turn

A player may defeat no more than one Monster during a turn.

## Action Cards

Action cards create an immediate effect described on the card.

When an Action card is played:

1. Resolve the effect written on the card completely.
2. Unless the card states otherwise, place it into the shared discard pile.
3. The player may continue their Action Phase.

Cards gained through an Action are immediately available for use.

For example, if a Draw Action gives the player another Action card, that new Action card may be played during the same turn.

Playing an Action card does not count as defeating a Monster and does not end the player's turn unless the card specifically states otherwise.

### Drawing Cards Through Actions

Some Action cards instruct the player to draw one or more cards.

The player always draws the full number of cards specified by the Action.

If the draw causes the player to exceed their maximum hand size, the Action finishes resolving first. The player then chooses and discards enough cards to return to the current hand limit.

After resolving the hand limit, the player may continue their Action Phase normally.

## Defeating a Monster

To defeat a regular Monster, the player must satisfy the requirements shown on that Monster.

Normally, this means playing the exact combination of Weapon cards shown on the Monster.

The required cards are placed into the shared discard pile unless a card says otherwise.

The player collects the defeated Monster and adds its point value to their score.

If multiple face-up Monsters can be defeated, the player chooses which one to defeat.

After defeating one Monster, that player's turn ends.

## Skipping

A player may choose to skip instead of defeating a Monster.

When a player skips, they draw 1 card from the shared Draw deck.

Drawing this card immediately ends their turn. They may not play the newly drawn card that turn.

If the player is already at their maximum hand size when they skip:

1. They first discard exactly 1 card.
2. They draw 1 card.
3. Their turn ends.

Voluntary discarding is not otherwise allowed.

## Ultimate Weapons

Ultimate Weapons are special cards capable of defeating Monsters without using their normal Weapon requirements.

A standard game contains exactly 1 Ultimate Weapon card.

Unless an Ultimate Weapon states otherwise:

* It may be used only after the player's Action Phase.
* Using it counts as defeating the player's one Monster for that turn.
* The defeated Monster is collected normally.
* The Ultimate Weapon is placed in the shared discard pile after use.
* The player's turn ends after the Monster is defeated.

### Black Hole

The core game's Ultimate Weapon is the Black Hole.

The Black Hole may be played by itself to defeat any face-up regular Monster, regardless of that Monster's normal Weapon requirements.

Only the Black Hole is discarded. The Monster's normal required Weapons remain in the player's hand.

The Black Hole cannot defeat the Sudden Death Monster.

## Recycling the Shared Draw Deck

When the shared Draw deck is depleted, the game performs a reset before play continues.

If 4 or more undefeated regular Monsters remain:

1. Remove the currently face-up Monsters from the board.
2. Place those Monsters on the bottom of the existing Monster deck.
3. Draw new Monsters from the top of the Monster deck to refill the board.
4. Shuffle the shared discard pile.
5. The shuffled discard pile becomes the new Draw deck.
6. Play continues normally.

If fewer than 4 undefeated regular Monsters remain, the face-up Monsters are not rotated out.

The shared discard pile is still shuffled to create a new Draw deck.

## End of the Regular Game

The regular game ends when every regular Monster has been defeated.

Players total the point values of all Monsters they collected.

If one player has the highest score, that player wins.

If two or more players are tied for the highest score, those tied players enter Sudden Death.

## Sudden Death

The game's designated Sudden Death Monster is placed face-up.

In the core game, this Monster is **The Infinity Beast**.

Before Sudden Death begins:

* Remove the Ultimate Weapon from the game, wherever it currently is.
* Each tied player's maximum hand size becomes 4 cards.
* If a tied player currently has more than 4 cards, they choose and discard cards until only 4 remain.

The tied players continue taking turns using the normal rules, including the Action Phase, with the following changes:

* Players may hold no more than 4 cards.
* Ultimate Weapons cannot be used.
* Action cards continue to function normally.
* Players may chain multiple Action cards during their Action Phase.
* Cards received from an Action may immediately be played.
* If a Draw Action causes a player's hand to exceed 4 cards, the full draw resolves first and the player then discards down to 4.
* Skipping still allows the player to draw 1 card and immediately ends their turn. If they already have 4 cards, they must discard 1 before drawing.

The first tied player to defeat the Sudden Death Monster immediately wins the game.

In the core set, The Infinity Beast requires the complete four-Weapon hand: Gun / Rifle + Spear + Grenade + Sword.

The Sudden Death Monster does not award normal points. Defeating it determines the winner directly.
