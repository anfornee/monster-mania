# Core Rules v1.0

## Objective

Players compete to defeat Monsters and earn points.

When all Monsters have been defeated, each player totals the point values of the Monsters they collected.

The player with the most points wins.

If two or more players are tied for the highest score, they enter Sudden Death against **The Infinity Beast**.

## Game Components

The game contains:

* Weapon cards
* Draw 1 cards
* Draw 2 cards
* Monster cards
* 1 Black Hole card
* 1 dedicated Sudden Death Monster: **The Infinity Beast**

## Shared Draw Deck

All players draw from one shared Draw deck.

At the beginning of the game, each player draws 3 cards.

Players may normally have no more than 5 cards in their hand.

Used Weapon cards and Draw cards are placed into a shared discard pile.

## Monster Board

Two Monster cards are normally face-up and available to defeat.

Each Monster shows the exact combination of Weapon cards required to defeat it.

Monster difficulty determines its point value:

* 1-point Monster requires 2 Weapon cards
* 2-point Monster requires 3 Weapon cards
* 3-point Monster requires 4 Weapon cards

When a Monster is defeated:

1. The player collects that Monster.
2. The Weapon cards used to defeat it are placed in the discard pile.
3. A new Monster is drawn to replace it.

A player may defeat only one Monster per turn.

## Final Three Monsters

When only 3 undefeated Monsters remain, all 3 are placed face-up on the board.

From this point onward, defeated Monsters are not replaced.

The game continues until all 3 have been defeated.

## Player Turn

On their turn, a player may:

* Play one or more Draw cards
* Defeat one Monster
* Skip their turn

A player may play any number of Draw cards during their turn, but may defeat no more than one Monster.

## Defeating a Monster

To defeat a Monster, the player must have the exact Weapon cards required by that Monster.

The required Weapon cards are played and placed into the shared discard pile.

The player collects the defeated Monster and adds its point value to their score.

After defeating one Monster, that player's turn ends.

## Skipping

A player may choose to skip instead of defeating a Monster.

When a player skips, they draw 1 card from the shared Draw deck.

Drawing this card immediately ends their turn. They may not play any cards afterward.

If the player already has 5 cards when they skip:

1. They must first discard 1 card.
2. They then draw 1 card.
3. Their turn ends.

## Draw 1

When a player plays a Draw 1 card:

1. The Draw 1 card is placed in the discard pile.
2. The player draws 1 card.
3. The player may continue their turn.

Playing a Draw card does not count as defeating a Monster or ending the player's turn.

## Draw 2

When a player plays a Draw 2 card:

1. The Draw 2 card is placed in the discard pile.
2. The player draws 2 cards.
3. The player may continue their turn.

Players may play multiple Draw cards during the same turn.

## Hand Limit After Draw Cards

A player's hand may normally contain no more than 5 cards.

When a Draw 1 or Draw 2 card causes the player to exceed 5 cards, they draw the full number of cards first.

They then choose which cards to discard until their hand contains no more than 5 cards.

After discarding, they may continue their turn normally.

## Black Hole

There is exactly one Black Hole card in the shared Draw deck.

The Black Hole may be played by itself to defeat any face-up Monster, regardless of that Monster's normal Weapon requirements.

After use, the Black Hole is placed into the shared discard pile.

The defeated Monster is collected normally.

Using the Black Hole counts as defeating the player's one Monster for that turn.

## Recycling the Shared Draw Deck

When the shared Draw deck is depleted, the game performs a reset before play continues.

If 4 or more undefeated Monsters remain:

1. Remove the currently face-up Monsters from the board.
2. Place those Monsters on the bottom of the Monster deck.
3. Draw new Monsters from the top of the Monster deck to refill the board.
4. Shuffle the shared discard pile.
5. The shuffled discard pile becomes the new Draw deck.
6. Play continues normally.

If fewer than 4 undefeated Monsters remain, the face-up Monsters are not rotated out.

The shared discard pile is still shuffled to create a new Draw deck.

## End of the Game

The normal game ends when every regular Monster has been defeated.

Players total the point values of all Monsters they collected.

The player with the highest score wins.

## Sudden Death

If two or more players are tied for the highest score, only those tied players enter Sudden Death.

The dedicated Sudden Death Monster, **The Infinity Beast**, is placed face-up.

Before Sudden Death begins:

* Remove the **Black Hole** from the game, wherever it currently is.
* Each tied player's maximum hand size becomes **4 cards**.
* If a tied player currently has more than 4 cards, they choose and discard cards until only 4 remain.

The tied players continue taking turns using the normal rules, with the following Sudden Death changes:

* Players may hold no more than 4 cards.
* The Black Hole cannot be used.
* Draw 1 and Draw 2 cards still function normally.
* Players may still play multiple Draw cards during their turn.
* If drawing causes a player's hand to exceed 4 cards, they draw the full amount first and then discard down to 4.
* Skipping still allows the player to draw 1 card and immediately ends their turn. If they already have 4 cards, they must discard 1 before drawing.

The first tied player to defeat **The Infinity Beast** immediately wins the game.

The Infinity Beast does not award points. Defeating it determines the winner directly.
